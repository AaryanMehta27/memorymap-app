# Privacy Policy

MemoryMap processes photos of private homes. Here is exactly how your data is handled:

- **Photos**: Captured photos are sent directly to Google Gemini API for object detection analysis. They are not stored on MemoryMap's servers. In-memory processing only.
- **Google Gemini**: Image analysis is performed via Google's Gemini API. Google's standard API data usage policies apply.
- **Your data**: Room layouts, tags, and photos you choose to save are stored in your private Supabase database. Access is restricted to your account only via Row Level Security.
- **Account deletion**: You can permanently delete all your data at any time from the Settings page.
